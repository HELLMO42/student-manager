Course Introduction

Welcome to the frontier of algorithmic design. This course investigates the theoretical foundations and practical applications of advanced algorithms. We move beyond basic data structures to explore the elegance of approximation, the power of randomized computation, and the rigor of complexity analysis.

Students will master the ability to not just solve problems, but to prove the efficiency and correctness of their solutions. From Network Flow to NP-Completeness, we provide the tools needed for high-level computational thinking.
